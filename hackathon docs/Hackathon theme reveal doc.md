**THEME: UNITED NATIONS SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS**

We kept this year's Social Impact standard super open to any UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) mostly because we wanted to give you total creative freedom to tackle whatever real world issue actually engages you (and okay, maybe a tiny bit because we were too lazy to come up with something super specific ourselves).  
Here are a few (totally not AI-generated) prompt ideas you could run with under the big SDG umbrella:

- **SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being) \- The Anti-Slouch Enforcer:** Build a quiet background tool that tracks your posture or screen distance and softly dims your display when you start turning into a human pretzel, saving your spine without spamming you with annoying pop-ups.
- **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) \- The "Did I Really Need This?" Button:** Create a one-tap browser extension or app that pauses online impulse buys, calculates how many hours of work that random item costs you, and tracks saved money to curb unnecessary waste.

- **SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) \- Quick-Tap Transit & Ramp Alerts:** Develop a dead-simple utility where commuters can report broken station elevators, blocked wheelchair ramps, or transit delays with just one single click to keep local travel accessible for everyone.

- **SDG 4 (Quality Education) \- Micro-Logic Arcade:** Design a fast, zero-instruction mini-game that teaches basic coding concepts, logic puzzles, or math tricks to younger students using simple mechanics and instant visual rewards.
